# Beachhead: Normandy 1944

August 1944. The Allies have broken out of Normandy. You run supply for three armies racing for Germany. The generals fight; you decide what reaches them.

Take Paris, Antwerp, Aachen and Metz before the deadline:

- **Everything comes ashore** at the beaches; Channel storms cut it; Cherbourg opens later; Antwerp only helps once its estuary is cleared
- **Trains** run on repaired track; the French railways were bombed before D-Day
- **Trucks** carry the gap from railhead to front, carrying less and burning more as it grows
- **Engineers** repair track and stations
- **Fighter-bombers** hit German build-ups that air recon reports
- **New divisions** land and go forward by train
- Resistance stiffens at the German border

Open `index.html` in a browser. No build step. A sister game to [Railhead](https://wizzard262.github.io/railhead/). Play Beachhead at https://wizzard262.github.io/beachhead-normandy/.
